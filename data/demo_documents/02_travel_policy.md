# Northstar Travel and Expense Policy (fictional demo document)

This fictional policy is sample content created only to demonstrate document search. It does not describe a real employer or reimbursement program.

## Before travel
Get written manager approval before booking business travel. Use the company booking portal when it is available. For domestic trips, lodging reimbursement is capped at USD 240 per night before taxes; ask Finance for an exception before booking if a destination has no available lodging within the cap.

## Meals and local transport
The sample daily meal allowance is USD 65 for a full travel day. Tips should be reasonable and itemized when the receipt supports it. Personal entertainment and companion expenses are not reimbursable.

## Filing a claim
Submit the expense report in the reimbursement portal within ten calendar days after returning. Attach itemized receipts for any individual expense over USD 25. If a receipt is unavailable, add a written explanation and the business purpose; Finance may request additional evidence.

## Reimbursement
A manager reviews the claim first, then Finance checks policy compliance. Approved claims are normally paid in the next regular reimbursement run. This example gives no guarantee about a real payment date.
