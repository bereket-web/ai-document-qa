# Northstar Data Handling Standard (fictional demo document)

This fictional standard is sample content created only to demonstrate document search. It does not describe a real company security program.

## Customer information
Store customer exports only in the approved encrypted workspace. Do not put customer data in personal email, public links, or unapproved AI tools. Downloaded working copies should be deleted within 24 hours after the task is complete.

## Sharing links
Use named recipients and the shortest practical expiration. Shared-folder links expire after seven days by default. Re-check access when a project closes and remove external collaborators who no longer need access.

## Security incidents
If you discover exposed credentials, customer data, or an unexpected public link, report it to the fictional Security Response team immediately and within one hour of discovery. Do not investigate by opening the exposed material. For an exposed credential, use the approved credential manager to rotate or revoke it after reporting.

## Exceptions and retention
A documented retention extension requires written approval from both the data owner and the fictional Privacy Lead. Record the justification and expiry date in the ticket. Exceptions are reviewed every 90 days.
