# Northstar People Handbook (fictional demo document)

This fictional handbook is sample content created only to demonstrate document search. It does not describe a real employer or policy.

## Hybrid work
Employees on hybrid schedules should work from the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Teams may agree on additional in-person days for planning. This shared schedule is intended to make collaboration predictable; individual arrangements should be discussed with a manager.

## Learning and development
Each full-time employee may claim up to USD 1,200 per calendar year for job-related courses, books, and professional certifications. A manager must approve a course before the employee purchases it. Reimbursement requires an itemized receipt and a short note explaining the work-related purpose. Unused funds do not roll over to the next year.

## Time away
For planned vacation, submit a request to your manager at least five business days before the first day away. For an unexpected illness, notify your manager before your normal workday begins when possible. Managers should record approved leave in the shared calendar.

## Equipment
Company equipment should be returned within ten business days after a role ends. Contact People Operations to arrange a prepaid shipping label when working remotely.
